Jungian Dream Interpretation

Help & support

Record a dream when you wake, and the app writes it down and reads it in the manner of Carl Jung, or roasts it, if you prefer Jungcore.

Contact

Questions, bugs or refunds: email keegancostley@gmail.com.

Dream tokens

  • You get 5 free tokens every month. One token is one reading, including one free reinterpretation within 30 days.
  • More tokens come in packs of 10 or 30 from Settings → Get more. They don't expire.
  • Bought tokens are tied to your account. Sign in with Apple in Settings so you keep them, and your journal, on a new phone.
  • For refunds of App Store purchases, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Your data

  • Your dreams are encrypted on your phone, so only your devices can read them. Read the privacy policy for what is collected and who processes it.
  • New phone? With iCloud Keychain on, your journal unlocks by itself. Otherwise, enter the 12-word recovery key from Settings → Recovery key on your old phone. Without either, an encrypted journal can't be recovered, by anyone.
  • To delete everything, go to Settings → Delete all my data. It can't be undone.

Not therapy

Interpretations are for reflection and entertainment, inspired by Jungian psychology. They aren't medical or psychological advice. If you're struggling, please talk to a professional or someone you trust.
