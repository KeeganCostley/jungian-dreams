Jungian Dream Interpretation

Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Jungian Dream Interpretation ("the app") is made by Keegan Costley, an individual developer in Australia. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it's shared with, and how to delete it.

What the app collects

  • Your dreams. Voice recordings you make (kept only until they're transcribed), the text transcribed from them, and anything you type (for example, context you add to a dream).
  • Interpretations. The readings, questions and rituals generated from your dreams, and any replies you write to dream figures.
  • Settings. Your chosen reading length and analyst style.
  • Account identifiers. A random account ID created when you first open the app. If you choose Sign in with Apple, your Apple ID's identifier and the email Apple shares (which may be a private relay address).
  • Purchases. Which token packs you bought and when, plus your token balance. Payment itself is handled by Apple; the app never sees your card details.
  • Usage counts. How many interpretations, transcriptions and narrations your account uses per day, to enforce fair-use limits.

The app does not collect your location, contacts or photos, does not show ads, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

How it's used

Only to provide the app: transcribing and interpreting your dreams, saving your journal, managing your dream tokens, and preventing abuse. Your data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Storage and security

Your dreams are encrypted on your phone before they're saved. The transcript, summary, symbols, notes, dialogues, every interpretation and the running notes the app keeps across your dreams are stored only as ciphertext. The key stays on your devices (in your iPhone's Keychain, synced through iCloud Keychain if you use it) and is never sent to the server, so the developer, Supabase and anyone with access to the database can't read them. Only the date, length and flags such as starred or lucid are stored unencrypted, so the journal can be sorted.

Voice recordings are encrypted before upload and deleted as soon as they've been transcribed.

To interpret a dream, its text has to be sent to the AI services above. It travels to them encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and is readable by them while they process it.

Settings has an optional 12-word recovery key. If you lose your phone and don't use iCloud Keychain, the recovery key is the only way to unlock your journal; without either, it can't be recovered, by anyone. AI service keys are held on the server, never in the app.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete all my data. This permanently deletes your recordings, dreams, interpretations, token balance and account. It cannot be undone, and purchased tokens are not refunded on deletion. If you can't access the app, email the address below to request deletion.

Age

The app is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at anyone younger and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated, and significant changes will be noted in the app.
